# WhereQ Universe v1.0.0 — The Galaxy Launch

> **Six timeless questions. Eight living products. One universe.**

Today we're launching **WhereQ Universe** — the public front door to a
collection of products built on a single idea: every meaningful product
answers one of six questions. WHERE, WHO, WHEN, WHY, WHAT, and HOW.
We turned that idea into a galaxy.

The launch marks the moment when the WhereQ ecosystem stops being a
collection of side projects and becomes a **system** — a system with a
foundation, a research station, a mission control, an incubation pipeline,
and a public voice.

---

## What's in this release

- **The WhereQ Universe landing page** (`/`) — an interactive SVG galaxy
  with an animated hero, scroll-driven reveals, and click-to-explore
  planets.
- **The 5W + 1H framework** — the architectural concept that turns six
  questions into a product roadmap.
- **Eight planets** — five foundational platforms on the inner orbit and
  three incubated products on the outer orbit.
- **The incubation system** — a six-step pipeline that explains how new
  planets are born and graduate into independent worlds.
- **The roadmap** — past, present, and future milestones of the
  universe.

---

## The 5W + 1H — the architectural idea

Journalists, engineers and detectives have relied on the same framework
for a century: **5W + 1H**. The WhereQ Universe takes it literally —
each question became a real platform with a real responsibility, and
together they form the foundation every new product is built upon.

| Question | Product | Metaphor | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WHERE** | [WhereQ](https://www.whereq.com) | The Spatial Engine | Maps, routing, places, geospatial intelligence |
| **WHO** | [KeyToMarvel](https://www.keytomarvel.com) | The Universal Passport | Identity, SSO, MFA, profiles, trust |
| **WHEN** | [Chroniq](https://www.chroniq.cc) | The Temporal Engine | Time, events, scheduling, booking *(coming soon)* |
| **WHY** | [WhereQ.ca](https://www.whereq.ca) | The Research Station | Experimentation, validation, prototyping, community feedback |
| **WHAT** | [whereq.github.io](https://whereq.github.io) | Mission Control | News, announcements, releases, ecosystem updates |
| **HOW** | The incubated | Proof the system works | FlowDesk, CCBus, CatoBigato |

> **One identity. One map. One clock. One lab. One voice. And a universe
> that keeps expanding.**

The five inner planets are **the foundation** — they are the questions
every product in the universe must answer. The three outer planets are
**proof** that the foundation works — they were incubated in the lab
and graduated into independent worlds.

## How new planets are born

Every product follows the same trajectory: it starts as a question
inside the 5W core, gets validated in the lab, and graduates into an
independent world.

```
01 — 5W Core
       Identity, location, time, validation and voice — ready to build on.
02 — Ideas
       A new question worth answering emerges.
03 — Validation
       Tested on WhereQ.ca with real community feedback.
04 — Prototype
       A working world takes shape in the lab.
05 — Production
       Hardened, launched, announced from mission control.
06 — Independent product
       Its own domain, its own orbit — still powered by the core.
```

### Graduates

Three products have completed the pipeline and are now in independent
orbits. Each one started in the lab, was validated with the community,
hardened into production, and announced from mission control:

- **FlowDesk** — Graduated as the universe's AI trading platform —
  built on all five foundations, fronted by NOVA.
- **CCBus** — Graduated as the Web3 & DeFi planet — token creation,
  presales and liquidity on three chains.
- **CatoBigato** — Graduated as the learning planet — vibe tutoring
  for K-12 and beyond.

## The map of dependencies

The galaxy is not flat. Every outer planet depends on a subset of the
inner planets. Hovering an inner planet in the live map highlights its
dependents; hovering an outer planet highlights its dependencies.

**Edges (in the live map)**:

- **FlowDesk** → WHO + WHERE + WHEN + WHY + WHAT
  _(depends on all five foundations)_
- **CCBus** → WHO + WHY + WHAT
- **CatoBigato** → WHO + WHEN + WHY + WHAT
- **WHERE** → WHO _(every place needs a visitor)_
- **WHEN** → WHO _(every event needs an attendee)_
- **WHY** → WHO _(every idea needs a critic)_
- **WHAT** → WHO _(every broadcast needs an audience)_

The pattern is striking: **WHO is the most depended-on planet in the
universe.** Without identity, none of the others can address a user.
KeyToMarvel is the load-bearing core of everything that ships.

## How to explore the universe

The interactive WhereQ Universe landing page is the recommended entry
point:

1. **Visit** [whereq.com](https://www.whereq.com) or
   [whereq.ca](https://www.whereq.ca) → click the globe icon to open
   the Universe.
2. **Scroll** through the origin story, the galaxy map, the
   architecture, the incubation system, and the roadmap.
3. **Click a planet** in the galaxy to open its detail panel — its
   metaphor, tagline, responsibilities, and a deep-dive link to the
   next section of the page.
4. **Hover a planet** to highlight its dependencies (inner orbit) or
   its dependents (outer orbit).
5. **Visit a planet's domain** to enter that world. Each is an
   independent application with its own features, design, and team.

The landing page works on desktop and mobile. On narrow screens the
galaxy zooms in to the active planet's detail panel rather than
showing the full SVG.

## What this release means

Until today, the WhereQ ecosystem was a **collection of products** —
useful individually but with no shared story. The WhereQ Universe is
the **story** that ties them together.

The 5W + 1H framework is not a marketing conceit. It is the
architectural principle that the lab uses to decide which products
to build, which to incubate, and which to graduate. The galaxy is
the visualization of that principle. The roadmap is the execution
plan.

If you are a new user, start at [whereq.com](https://www.whereq.com)
(WHERE) and follow the breadcrumbs to the planet that solves the
problem you have today. If you are a builder, start at
[whereq.ca](https://www.whereq.ca) (WHY) and see what the lab is
incubating next. If you just want to keep tabs, subscribe to
[whereq.github.io](https://whereq.github.io) (WHAT) — that is where
the next announcement will land.
